Hand fun facts
	How many joints, ligaments, muscles, and nerves do you think are in your hand?
		The hands have 29 major joints, at least 123 ligaments, 34 muscles, 48 nerves and 30 arteries.
	True or false? You have no muscles that cross your finger joints. 
		True! Movements of the finger joints (DIP and PIP) are generated by tendons of muscles in the forearm.
	If you had to choose one finger to be chopped off, which one should you select?
		Hand surgeons say that the index finger is the best one to lose—if you had a choice. The index finger is needed the least during typical functional movements.
	Which finger tends to be longer? Your ring finger or your index finger?
		Men tend to have longer ring fingers than index fingers while women tend to have longer index fingers.
	How many different muscles control your thumb?
		Nine individual muscles control thumb movements.

Flexibility Assessment
	Finger Flexors 
		Keep the elbow and fingers fully extended, and then extend the wrist without radial or ulnar deviation and keeping the fingers extended.
		For normal flexibility, the wrist should be able to extend through its full range.
	Finger Extensors
		Keep the elbow extended and fingers flexed, and then flex the wrist without radial or ulnar deviation and keeping the fingers flexed.
		For normal flexibility, the wrist should be able to flex through its full range.
