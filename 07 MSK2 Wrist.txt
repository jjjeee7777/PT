Flexibility Assessment
	Flexion
		Keep the elbow extended, with the fingers and thumb relaxed. Extend the wrist without radial or ulnar deviation.
		For normal flexibility, the wrist should be able to extend through its full range. The fingers may flex.
	Extension
		Keep the elbow extended, with the fingers and thumb relaxed. Flex the wrist without radial or ulnar deviation.
		For normal flexibility, the wrist should be able to flex through its full range. The fingers may extend.
