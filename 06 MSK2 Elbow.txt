Elbow Comprehensive
////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////
////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////

Like the knee, because the elbow is a hinge joint, elbow flexion and extension should be documented as a continuum, not as separate measures.
	I.e.:  0°-145°
All wrist motions should also ALWAYS be assessed when examining the elbow

MMTs
	Elbow Flexion
		To focus on biceps, place the wrist in full supination
		For brachioradialis, place the wrist half-way between supination and pronation
		TO focus on the Brachialis place the wrist in full pronation
	Elbow extension (triceps)
	Pronation & Supination

Flexibility Assessment
	Assessment of flexibility in the upper extremity consists of determining if there is normal length of the following multiple-joint muscles
		Forearm and Hand 
			Long wrist flexors and extensors 
			Finger flexors and extensors
