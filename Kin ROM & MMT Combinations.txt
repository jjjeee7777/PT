                    Sources of ROM Limitation
--------------------------------------------------------------------
COLUMN 1
    ROM Limitations Present
COLUMN 2
    Possible Source of ROM Limitation

1. Pain and/or limitation of AROM only - 1. Muscle and tendon injury

2. Pain and/or limitation of both AROM & PROM - 2. Injury to joint capsule, ligaments, articular cartilage; possibly also muscle/tendon injury

2.b. Pain at end ranges of both R AROM and PROM. Ranges are normal - Contractile: Tendon stretching at end of motion (tendon injury).

3. Pain and/or limitation of only PROM - 3. Not possible in theory

4. Limitation of AROM only without pain - 4. Weak muscles
